# Paper Mario PAL Alpha 0.4.5 RC

This release candidate is based on PAL Alpha 0.4 and adds the generic texture
cache import-dimensions fix and the Quizmo native-matrix correction. The prior
PAL renderer, language/menu, sampler, TEXEL1, save-path, palette/Tattle, and
fragment-rotation corrections remain in the base.

The candidate intentionally excludes the M2 diagnostic capture overlay. Its
Onion app identity is `PaperMarioPALAlpha045RC`, with an independent `data/`
directory. No ROM, save file, trace, or diagnostic evidence is packaged.
